excerpt: >-
  We determine the optimal constant in Klartag's reduction of the slicing problem to centrally symmetric convex bodies.
  The argument compares the isotropic constant of a convex body with that of a Ball body built from its covariogram,
  using sharp inclusions within Ball's family for functions with stronger concavity than log-concavity.
