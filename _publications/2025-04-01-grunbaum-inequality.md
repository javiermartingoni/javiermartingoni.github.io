Grünbaum's inequality gives a sharp lower bound for the proportion of a convex body's volume contained in a halfspace whose boundary passes through its centroid. We extend this result to a one-parameter family of $r$-powered centroids by proving a more general inequality for concave functions. This functional framework also recovers the Minkowski–Radon inequality relating the centroid to supporting hyperplanes, as well as a section-volume inequality established independently by Makai Jr. and Martini, and by Fradelizi.
